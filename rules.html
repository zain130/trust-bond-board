Rules: how the board works

TopicRule
Trust BondRefundable deposit (Rs 2,000–20,000) a shop places to rank. Shown publicly on every listing. It is amanat, not our revenue.
RankInside a category, rank = monthly boost bid (highest first). Ties go to the larger bond, then the earlier listing.
BoostMonthly, non-refundable. To climb back after being outbid, pay only the difference to your new total.
OutbidWhen a rival outbids you, you drop a rank. You may raise your boost at any time to reclaim it.
Verification feeOne-time Rs 500–1,000 per shop on approval. First 30 shops launch free.
Bond fee10% of the bond is kept as platform fee. The rest is refunded on clean exit.
ComplaintsBuyer files with payment proof. Seller gets 7 days to resolve. Unresolved = refund from bond, bond reduced, shop demoted or removed.
Bond vs order sizeA shop's bond must stay above its average order value, or fraud would still pay. Shops below that line lose rank until they top up.
Sponsored labelsEvery ranked position is a paid position. Nothing here is an organic or editorial ranking.
HonestyNo fake shops, no fake counters, no fake reviews. One caught lie ends a shop's listing permanently.

v0 demo: listings on this version are sample data. Real bonds, boosts and disputes activate with the database launch (Phase 2).

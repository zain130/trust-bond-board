Rules, Policy aur Terms

Ye rules aam insaan ki zuban me likhe hain. Koi wakeel wali mushkil zuban nahi, taake buyer aur seller dono ko saaf pata ho ke un ke haq kya hain aur zimmedari kya hai.

Trust Bond Board kya hai?

Ye un online shops ki list hai jinhon ne apna Trust Bond hamare pas jama karwaya hai. Bond ka matlab ye hai ke shop ne apni neeyat ka saboot diya hai: agar wo buyer ke saath ghalat karegi to buyer ko usi bond se refund milega. Rank khareedna nahi, bharosa dikhana hai.

Buyer ke liye: aap ka paisa kaise mehfooz hai

  • Bond Protected badge sirf verified shops ko milta hai. Ye badge usi shop par dikhta hai jis ka bond payment admin ne khud confirm kiya ho. Badge ke baghair shop ka matlab hai ke us ka bond abhi verify nahi hua.
  • Order WhatsApp par hota hai. Board khud payment nahi leta. Aap seller se WhatsApp par baat kar ke order karte hain, aur payment ka saboot (receipt aur chat) apne pas rakhen. Ye saboot complaint ke waqt kaam aayega.
  • Fraud ho to bond se refund. Agar shop payment le kar cheez na bheje ya ghalat cheez bheje, to complaint karen. Seller ko 7 din milenge. Hal na hua to aap ko shop ke bond se refund milega, bond ki raqam ki had tak.
  • Board ka waada bond ki had tak hai. Refund sirf us bond se ho sakta hai jo shop ne jama karwaya hai. Is se zyada raqam ka waada board nahi karta.

Seller ke liye: shop list karne ka poora tareeqa

  1. Form bharen: List Your Shop page par shop ka naam, category, WhatsApp number, sheher, bond amount aur ek line me tafseel likhen. Shop ka link (Instagram, Facebook ya website) ho to den, warna khali chhor den, koi masla nahi.
  2. Verification: Admin aap se WhatsApp par rabta karega aur bond payment confirm karega. Payment confirm hone ke baad hi shop approve hogi.
  3. PIN milega: Approval ke baad aap ko aik management PIN WhatsApp par diya jayega. Is PIN se aap manage page par apni shop khol sakenge.
  4. Products list karen: Manage page par product ka naam, price, tag aur tasveer ka link den, Add dabayen. Product foran aap ki shop page par live ho jayega. Kisi product ko badalna ho to Edit, hatana ho to Delete dabayen.
  5. Rank banayen: Rank total boost se banti hai. Aap ka diya hua boost hamesha aap ke total me rehta hai, kabhi khatam nahi hota. Koi aap se upar nikal jaye to sirf farq ada kar ke rank wapas le sakte hain, dobara poori raqam nahi deni hoti.

Rank kaise banti hai

Har category ke andar rank total boost se banti hai: jis shop ka total boost zyada ho wo upar. Agar do shops ka total barabar ho to jis ka bond bara hai wo upar. Phir bhi barabar ho to jo shop pehle list hui wo upar. Top position claim karne ke liye top wali raqam se sirf Rs 1,000 zyada total chahiye. Jo shop pehli dafa top lena chahti hai wo poori raqam degi. Jis shop ne pehle kabhi boost diya hua hai, us ki pehli raqam count hogi, us ko sirf farq dena hoga. Misal: aap ki shop top par hai aur aap ne 20,000 diye hue hain. Kisi nayi shop ne 21,000 de kar top le liya aur aap dosre number par aa gaye. Ab dobara top par aane ke liye aap 22,000 nahi denge, sirf 2,000 denge, kyunke aap ke pehle 20,000 count honge (20,000 + 2,000 = 22,000).

Complaint aur refund ka tareeqa

  1. Complaint karen: Complaint page par shop, apna WhatsApp number, order amount aur jo hua wo likhen. Payment receipt aur chat ke screenshots apne pas ready rakhen, admin maang sakta hai.
  2. Seller ko 7 din: Seller ko 7 din milenge ke wo masla hal kare: ya cheez bheje, ya badle, ya refund de.
  3. Hal ho gaya to khatam: Dono raazi ho jayen to complaint band kar di jayegi.
  4. Hal na hua to bond se refund: 7 din me hal na ho to buyer ko shop ke bond se refund diya jayega, bond ki raqam ki had tak. Shop ka bond kam ho jayega aur shop rank me neeche chali jayegi ya remove ho jayegi.
  5. Jhooti complaint: Jo buyer jhooti complaint karega us ko block kar diya jayega. Saboot ke baghair complaint aage nahi barhegi.

Ye chezen mana hain

  • Naqli (replica) cheez ko asli keh kar bechna.
  • Ghalat price, ghalat tafseel ya kisi aur ki tasveer apni keh kar lagana.
  • Illegal ya qanoon ke khilaf koi cheez bechna.
  • Buyer ko dhoka dena, payment le kar ghaib ho jana.
  • Fake reviews, fake orders ya fake followers dikha kar rank lena.

Shop kab remove hogi

  • Fraud sabit ho jaye to shop foran remove aur bond se refund.
  • Bond khatam ho jaye aur shop top-up na kare to rank khatam, phir listing khatam.
  • Baar baar delivery na karna ya baar baar complaints aana.
  • Rules torhna ya jhooti maloomat dena. Aik pakri gayi jhooti baat listing khatam kar sakti hai, kyunke ye bharose ka karobar hai.

Terms & Conditions (seedhi zuban me)

  • Board sirf listing aur protection ka zariya hai. Bechne wala seller hai, khareedne wala buyer hai. Order un ke darmiyan WhatsApp par hota hai.
  • Board ka waada sirf ye hai ke verified shop ka bond hamare pas mehfooz hai aur dispute ke usoolon ke mutabiq us se refund hoga, bond ki had tak.
  • Ghalat maloomat dene par listing baghair notice ke remove ki ja sakti hai, aur bond se pending refunds pehle poore kiye jayenge.
  • Bond amanat hai. Ye board ki kamai nahi hai aur is par koi sood ya munafa nahi diya jata.
  • Rules ya fees badle to board par notice diya jayega. Purani listings par naye rules sirf notice ke baad laagu honge.

Privacy

Aap ka WhatsApp number sirf order aur complaint ke liye use hoga. Ye number kisi ko becha ya share nahi kiya jayega. Complaint ki public tafseel me buyer ka naam chhupaya jayega.

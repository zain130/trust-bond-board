Kaise kaam karta hai

Teen seedhe steps, koi chhupi hui shart nahi.

1

Shop bond jama karwati hai

Rs 2,000 se 20,000 tak refundable bond. Ye amanat hai, hamari kamai nahi.

2

Total boost se rank milti hai

Rank aap ke total boost se banti hai, jo aik dafa di gayi raqam hamesha aap ke total me rehti hai. Top position ke liye top wali raqam se sirf Rs 1,000 zyada total chahiye. Pehli dafa poori raqam, dobara sirf farq.

3

Fraud par bond se refund

Complaint par seller ko 7 din milte hain. Hal na ho to bond se buyer ko refund.

Trust Bond kya hai?

Trust Bond shop ki neeyat ka saboot hai. Shop bond jama karwati hai, admin payment verify karta hai, phir shop board par aati hai. Agar shop buyer ke saath ghalat kare to buyer ko usi bond se refund milta hai, bond ki raqam ki had tak. Bond amanat hai, refundable hai, aur board ki kamai nahi.

Poori policy parhen

ShoppingOye v0 (demo). Bond money is held as amanat and is refundable on clean exit minus a 10% platform fee. Boost is a one-time cumulative payment, non-refundable. A shop's earlier boost always counts toward its total. Paid ranks are always labelled.
